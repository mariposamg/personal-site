About me

For more than 10 years I have built data and AI platforms for industrial, energy, logistics, utilities and consumer-goods organizations across Europe and Africa — from first MVP to production, and from hands-on code to leading squads and global data factories.

Today my focus is where data engineering meets generative AI: RAG pipelines and their evaluation, agentic architectures, and "AI for BI" on Microsoft Fabric. I care about governance, FinOps and Responsible AI by design, and about helping teams grow — as a trainer and through the community.
